📝 Planning Notes

🏝️ Poolside Planning Guide

Complete guide for your Holiday Tiki Luxe pool party

🍹

Food & Beverage Stations

Tiki Bar Setup

  • Location: Shaded area with easy pool access
  • Size: 8-10ft bar with bamboo décor
  • Signature Drinks: Mai Tai, Piña Colada, Tropical Punch
  • Mocktails: Virgin Mojito, Tropical Sunrise, Coconut Water Cooler
  • Garnish Station: Fresh pineapple, coconut, mint, orchids
  • Ice & Coolers: Multiple coolers with crushed ice

Poolside Food Stations

  • Main Station: Hawaiian BBQ (kalua pork, grilled pineapple, teriyaki skewers)
  • Appetizer Table: Tropical fruit platters, coconut shrimp, poke bowls
  • Dessert Station: Haupia (coconut pudding), pineapple upside-down cake, tropical sorbet
  • Setup Notes: Keep food away from pool splash zone, use chafing dishes
  • Dietary Options: Clear labels for vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free items

Hydration & Refreshments

  • Water Station: Infused water (cucumber-mint, citrus-berry)
  • Placement: Multiple stations around pool deck
  • Drinkware: Eco-friendly cups with guest markers
💡

Lighting Design & Ambiance

Pool Lighting (3-5 PM)

  • Natural Light: Take advantage of afternoon sun
  • Shade Options: Umbrellas, canopies for sun-sensitive guests
  • Early Transition: Test lights at 4:30 PM for sunset readiness

Evening Lighting (5-8 PM)

  • Underwater LEDs: Color-changing pool lights (tropical blues, greens)
  • Tiki Torches: Perimeter lighting for tropical ambiance
  • String Lights: Warm white Edison bulbs overhead
  • Lanterns: Floating paper lanterns in pool (battery-operated)
  • Pathway Lights: Solar stakes for walkways and stairs
  • Accent Lighting: Uplighting for palm trees and features
  • Bar Lighting: LED strips under bar counter

Safety Lighting

  • Pool Edge: Subtle lighting to mark boundaries
  • Steps & Entry: Well-lit entry points
  • Emergency: Keep flashlights accessible

Special Effects

  • Sparkle Elements: Battery-operated fairy lights in foliage
  • Candles: Floating candles (LED for safety)
  • Projections: Optional snowflake projector for Christmas touch
🌡️

Temperature & Comfort Control

Pool Temperature

  • Target Temp: 82-84°F (28-29°C)
  • Heat Schedule: Start heating 24-48 hours before party
  • Monitoring: Check temp every 4 hours leading up to event
  • Backup Plan: Have pool heater serviced one week prior

Air Temperature Management

  • Heating Options: Outdoor propane heaters (4-6 units)
  • Placement: Around seating areas, not near pool edge
  • Fire Pits: 2-3 fire pit tables for warmth and ambiance
  • Timing: Start heaters 30 minutes before guests arrive
  • Wind Breaks: Decorative screens to block December breeze

Guest Comfort Essentials

  • Towels: Heated towel rack with luxury towels
  • Robes: Plush robes available in changing areas
  • Blankets: Decorative throw blankets on lounge chairs
  • Slippers: Disposable spa slippers available
  • Hot Beverages: Coffee, hot cocoa station after 6 PM

Weather Contingencies

  • Rain Plan: Retractable awning or large tent on standby
  • Temperature Drop: Extra heaters in storage
  • Wind: Secure all decorations and light items
  • Communication: Send weather update to guests 24 hours before

December-Specific Considerations

  • Sunset Time: ~5:15 PM (plan lighting accordingly)
  • Temperature Range: Expect 55-65°F outdoor temps
  • Dress Code Note: Remind guests to bring layers
  • Heated Spaces: Designate warm indoor space for breaks
⏰

Day-Of Setup Timeline

Morning Setup (8 AM - 12 PM)

  • 8:00 AM: Pool cleaning and final chemical check
  • 9:00 AM: Set up tiki bar structure and furniture
  • 10:00 AM: Install lighting and test all systems
  • 11:00 AM: Arrange decorations and tropical foliage

Afternoon Prep (12 PM - 3 PM)

  • 12:00 PM: Verify pool temperature
  • 1:00 PM: Set up food and beverage stations
  • 2:00 PM: Final décor touches and sparkle elements
  • 2:30 PM: Test music system and playlist

Pre-Party Final Check (2:45 PM)

  • Pool temperature and cleanliness
  • All lighting operational
  • Heaters positioned and ready
  • Bar fully stocked
  • Towels and robes in place
  • Safety equipment accessible
  • Music queued up
🛟

Safety & Logistics

Pool Safety

  • Lifeguard: Consider hiring certified lifeguard for 3-8 PM
  • Safety Equipment: Life rings, reaching pole, first aid kit
  • Signage: Pool rules and depth markers clearly visible
  • Supervision: Designate 2-3 adults to monitor pool area
  • Emergency Plan: Post emergency numbers, nearest hospital info

Changing & Restroom Facilities

  • Changing Rooms: 2 cabanas with privacy screens
  • Restrooms: Clean, stocked, easily accessible
  • Amenities: Hair dryers, toiletries, sunscreen
  • Storage: Lockers or baskets for personal items

Alcohol Management

  • Wristbands: Different colors for 21+ and under 21
  • Bartender Training: Brief on responsible service
  • Water Promotion: Encourage hydration between drinks
  • Ride Options: Rideshare info posted, designated driver list

Logistics Checklist

  • Parking: Designate parking areas, consider valet
  • Entry Point: Clear signage to pool area
  • Music Volume: Consider neighbors, set cutoff time
  • Trash & Recycling: Multiple stations, frequent emptying
  • Cleanup Crew: Schedule for during and after party
